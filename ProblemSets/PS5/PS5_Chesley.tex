\section{API - US Energy Information Administration}
This data is over the CO2 emissions in millions of metric tons from 1949-2024. It separates the emissions by fuel type and total CO2 emissions. I found it interesting that we peaked in 2007 with 6015.262 million metric tons released and have been on a decline since. My hypothesis is that this was due to stronger emission guidelines which have since been repealed. I used the EIA package on R.  This will be much more useful to my project and the EIA has even more data that breaks emissions down by state. This could be interesting to breakdown the difference in emissions for states with and without certain environmental protection laws.
