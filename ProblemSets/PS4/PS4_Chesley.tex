\section{Data Sources}

In light of recent news, I would like to change my topic to how the global emission standards have effected the climate crisis. With the United States reeling back all emission standards, I want to take a closer look into what this impact has been in the past and what it possibly could be. To do this, I have found different data sets to analyze.

Firstly is the Emissions Database for Global Atmospheric Research by the European Union. This has tables with data on total emissions, emissions per capita, and emissions per GDP. It includes GHG data (fossil fuels) and CO2 data. 

Data.gov has relevant data sets over emissions, air quality, and temperatures over time that could be helpful in drawing a link between rising emissions and climate change. They also have simulations for future climate impacts.

I would really like to see the policy effect of having emission standards. While it is noted that in the current research, it reduces emissions by roughly 5 percent, I would want to put this alongside the current air quality, wildfire hazards, and forecast simulations to see if this is accurate.
